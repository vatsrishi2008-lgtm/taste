Where the season sets the table.

VELORA is a small dining room built on one idea: a meal should follow the year, not the other way around. Our menu is rewritten weekly around produce from Hudson Valley farms, day boats off Montauk and purveyors we have known for years.

Everything that can be cooked over fire is. Live oak, applewood and a hearth at the center of the room.

Chef Adrian Vale

Adrian grew up between the Hudson Valley and the coast of Maine, then spent a decade cooking in Portland, Chicago and Copenhagen before coming home to New York. His aim is simple: food that tastes like where it came from.

At VELORA he works with a small brigade and a wood-fired hearth. No foams, no tweezers for show. Fire, salt, acid and time.

A great dinner isn't a performance. It's a conversation between a farmer, a cook and the person at the table.

Adrian Vale, Executive Chef and Partner
